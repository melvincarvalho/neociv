# The commission

> this is great now do civ

Tenth game in the series. Same method as the first nine: one owner builds a
single-file tribute, deterministic `?shot=` captures, four harsh sub-agent
critics (composition, game-feel, HUD, and a Civilization-fidelity judge),
honest scores, publish. The proof harness's next species: **empires as
theorems** — a solution bot that must found, research, expand and conquer;
a null civ that must fall; ablations proving science and expansion are each
load-bearing; and a mechanism proof for every law from the food box to the
city walls.
